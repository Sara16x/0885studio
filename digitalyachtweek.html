Digital Yacht Week

Brand Identity & Creative Direction | 2026

Digital Yacht Week is the first global digital student competition focused on future boating concepts. Students exhibit projects built entirely in 3D, and the identity translates the codes of the nautical world into a contemporary design system that treats the screen as an instrument rather than a stage.

The goal

Build an institutional, unmistakably nautical language that the user reads at a glance.

The goal

The palette is lifted directly from naval displays, working as a functional set rather than a decorative one. ChartBlue sets the deep field, GridGrey carries the structure, Offwhite Nautical defines the operating surface, and Signal is reserved for active states. The grid quotes the quadrant logic of nautical charts and carries every layout. Image treatment is hyperrealistic by design, calibrated to place the viewer onboard rather than at a distance. The photographic narrative is anchored to three moments of the day, sunrise, midday, and sunset, the hours when a yacht is best lived and best seen, and the same hours that govern both the render schedule and the publishing rhythm.
