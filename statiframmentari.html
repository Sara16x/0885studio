Stati Frammentari

Editorial design | 2024

"Stati Frammentari" is an editorial project that explores Rome's urban landscape along the Centocelle - Termini Laziale tram line. This journey transforms the route into an abstract visual alphabet, synthesizing the tapestry of neighborhoods into signs.
