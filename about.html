Sara Cannone

Visual Designer · 0885studio · Italy

0885studio is the visual design practice of Sara Cannone. The studio works across brand identity, editorial design, motion graphics and interactive projects — building visual languages that translate ideas into clear, memorable experiences.

Approach

Each project starts with a conversation — about the people involved, the place it comes from, and the audience it wants to reach. Through research, sketching and iteration, the work is shaped to feel both grounded and surprising. The goal is to build identities, books, posters and motion pieces that hold attention and reward a closer look.
