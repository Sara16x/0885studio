Sara Cannone

Creative Director and Visual Designer · 0885archive · Italy

0885archive is a working archive of visual design projects developed across brand identity, editorial, motion and digital exhibition. It collects what gets made, what gets tested, and what survives the process. The archive is led by Sara Cannone, who builds these projects together with people who have a story worth telling.

Approach

Every project arrives with its own logic and deserves to be treated that way: listening before deciding, questioning before delivering. Research and experimenting are the foundation of the first phase, iteration is the engine, and the brief is a starting point rather than a ceiling. The goal is work that holds up when the trends move on.
