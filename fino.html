Fino

Brand identity | 2024

Fino is a visual identity project of an Apulian family farm.
The goal is the storytelling of a tradition of passion, quality and respect for the production of Fino Extra Virgin Olive Oil.

The essence

A balanced dance between delicacy and character that captures your senses. Fino Extra Virgin Olive Oil combines its Apulian roots with the emotions of those who are tasting it.
